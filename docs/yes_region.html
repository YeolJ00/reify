the finding

A brass pot given μ = 0.74 — rubber grip, on metal — scores p(yes) = 0.983 on the tilt probe. A wooden bowl at ρ = 309 kg/m³, a third the density of balsa, scores 0.965. The deliberately-wrong θ average 0.804 against 0.799 for everything else: no separation at all.

So the yes-region cannot define an asset by itself. Sampling it and keeping whatever passes would ship a brass pot that grips like a rubber eraser. This is the answer to the feasibility framing — feasibility is only as good as the acceptance test, and this acceptance test admits nearly everything offered.
