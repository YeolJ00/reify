A correction

I called rule 4 clean on too little data

After the single fit I reported ρ(score, motion magnitude) = −0.219 and −0.292 as “the first clean rule-4 result in this project”. Across all 240 evaluations here it is −0.884, with ρ(score, bounce count) = −0.771. The confound was present throughout; the earlier figure was a 28-point sample.

That is the same mistake as reading the cookbook prompt’s 6.2-point dynamic range as a result before checking what it correlated with. Small samples on the favourable side of a question deserve more suspicion than I gave them.

What this settles

  • The single run’s +0.125 was noise. Seeds move the duck by 0.182, larger than the effect it claimed.
  • The band calibration was right. The judge’s implied prior over restitution has a proper interior mode but does not condition on material — and the optimiser confirms it from the other direction.
  • Running it was still correct. A grid correlation and an optimiser’s path are different measurements; only this one could settle which was right, and it cost one overnight job.
  • What is left is the evidence source, not the machinery. Newton, the Cycles harness, the motion guard, the judges, CEM, SPSA and the int8 reasoner path all work. None of them can extract material information that is not in the signal.
