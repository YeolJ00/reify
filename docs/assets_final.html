What this is and is not

It is a set of assets whose motion a video-prior model rates as consistent with what they are made of, reached by optimising against that model and nothing else. For authored content with no footage and no ground truth, that is the available definition of “moves right”.
It is not a recovery of true friction, and the fit does not identify one. Across 8 SPSA iterations the gradient sign flips about half the time (2/8, 3/8, 4/8), so the optimiser is wandering inside a region the judge rates highly rather than climbing to a point. Read honestly, that means many μ in this region are plausible and the fit returns one of them — a wide posterior, reported as such, not a point estimate. Independent restarts would land elsewhere in the same region.
