what four hours bought

Nothing moved. Pooled 49% against v1's 58% and v2's 50% — all chance. The three new probes did not separate: stack 5/6 on the pot but 1/6 on the bowl, spin 3/6 and 4/6, shove 3/6 and 2/6. No probe is consistently informative across both objects.

Mass got worse, not better. Brass density across runs: 244 → 188 → 356 kg/m³, against a true ~8500. Three collision variants and exclusive ownership of ρ under the mask did not help. The slow-impact hypothesis — that 1.5 m/s was too fast for the judge to resolve — is not supported: collide_slow scored 3/6 and 2/6, no better than the fast one.

This is the sixth structural improvement with no change in outcome. Every one was a real defect, correctly identified and correctly fixed. The gradient stayed at chance throughout.
