Each parameter's path from its starting value, the probe that can see it, and the simulation at the first and final step. Curves rather than tables, and two clips per probe rather than every iteration.

Which probe moved the fit

Each square is one iteration's contribution from that probe — green positive, red negative, size by magnitude. A probe carrying signal shows one colour; a probe carrying noise alternates.

what the squares say

Tilt is the closest to one-coloured (5/6 and 4/6 the same sign). Collide is the largest and alternates — median |dy| 1.72 on the brass pot against tilt's 0.40, at 3/6. It contributes magnitude, not direction, so an unweighted sum lets it dominate every step.

Visible in the numbers above: the brass pot's density lands at 244 kg/m³ against ~8500 for brass. The collision probe exists specifically to pin density, and it is the least reliable term — so ρ drifted rather than converged. The degeneracy is not broken in practice even though the probe that should break it is present.
