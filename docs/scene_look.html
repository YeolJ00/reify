The grey was our own floor

A 40 m plane sits under the table to catch shadows. It is set near-black, but the HDRI lights it to mid-grey and it occludes the environment's own ground, so the street vanishes below the horizon and is replaced by a flat slab. Making it a shadow catcher — invisible to camera, still receiving contact shadow — fixes both at once.

This also matters beyond looks. The table now sits in a scene with depth cues and a real ground plane rather than floating above a grey void, which is the kind of context a judge uses to read contact and orientation.
